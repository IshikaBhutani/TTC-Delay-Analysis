⚡ Key Findings

YU (Yonge-University) subway line had the most delays of any single route — 10,293 incidents — more than 1.6× the next highest route (BD at 6,424).

Mechanical failures are the #1 cause of delays at 28.6% of all incidents (41,482 events), pointing to fleet maintenance as a key operational priority.

Late night hours (2–4 AM) have the highest average delay duration (28–32 min), likely due to reduced crew availability and fewer backup vehicles.

Friday is the worst day for delay frequency with 22,975 incidents. Weekends surprisingly have fewer delays than weekdays.

Bus accounts for 71% of all delay incidents (103,452 of 145,212), making it the highest-priority mode for operational improvements.

2024 saw more delays than 2023 in the first half of the year (Jan–Jun), while 2023 had more in Q4 — suggesting seasonal and operational shifts worth investigating.
